# Problem Statement

## SIH Number
SIH25031

## Title
Crowdsourced Civic Issue Reporting and Resolution System

## Organization
Government of Jharkhand

## Category
Software

## Theme
Clean & Green Technology

## Team Interpretation
The Government of Jharkhand wants a unified platform to bridge the communication gap between citizens and municipal bodies. Instead of dealing with multiple helplines, paper complaints, or scattered social media tags, citizens need one clear digital channel to report local issues. The authorities need a system that organizes these reports automatically so they can focus on solving them rather than sorting them.

## Problem Understanding
Civic issues like potholes, broken streetlights, water leakage, and overflowing garbage are common but often go unreported or unresolved due to a lack of a streamlined reporting mechanism. A crowdsourced system allows any citizen to become the "eyes and ears" of the city. The core challenge is not just collecting reports, but routing them accurately to the correct department, verifying their authenticity, and providing transparent updates back to the citizen.

## Target Users
1. **Citizens:** Everyday residents who encounter civic issues and want a quick, easy way to report them without bureaucratic friction.
2. **Municipal Authorities / Department Heads:** Officials who need to triage, assign, and monitor the resolution of these issues.
3. **On-ground Workers:** The personnel who receive the task, travel to the location, physically fix the issue, and provide proof of resolution.

## Existing Problems / Pain Points
- **For Citizens:** No clear way to report issues, lack of follow-up or status tracking, feeling ignored by the system, complex grievance filing processes.
- **For Authorities:** Duplicate complaints for the same issue, lack of accurate location data (which wastes time), difficulty in prioritizing severe issues, manual sorting of complaints to correct departments, and lack of data analytics to identify recurring problem zones.

## Constraints
- **Accessibility:** Must be mobile-first and incredibly easy to use (inclusive for varying technical literacy).
- **Location Accuracy:** Geo-tagging must be precise and mandatory for actionable reports.
- **Spam / Fake Reports:** The system needs a way to filter out fake, malicious, or duplicate reports.
- **Multilingual Support:** Must support local languages (e.g., Hindi, English) for broader reach in Jharkhand.
- **Bandwidth/Network:** Should work reasonably well even on slower 3G/4G networks common in some areas.
